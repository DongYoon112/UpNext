Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026. This policy covers the UpNext mobile app and the UpNext backend that serves it.

Who is responsible

UpNext is built and operated by an individual developer. For any privacy question, or to ask what data is held about you, email gucciyd248@gmail.com.

What UpNext collects

Your account

  • Email address and password. You create an account with an email address and a password. The password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is stored only as a hash — UpNext never sees or stores your password in readable form, and no one at UpNext can look it up.
  • Display name — the name you type at sign-up.
  • Role — student, recruiter, or organizer, and the date your account was created.

Your queue activity

  • Which booths you joined and your place in each queue, as a ticket number.
  • Timestamps for what happened to each ticket: when you joined, when you were called, when you checked in, when the conversation started and finished, and if the ticket was cancelled or expired — including the reason a ticket was cancelled.
  • Which recruiter called you or checked you in.

If you are a recruiter or organizer

  • Which booths you are a member of, and the role you hold at that booth.
  • Content you create in the app: event name, date and location; company name and description; booth table label and settings.

Notifications

  • If you turn on queue notifications, UpNext stores a push token for that device (an anonymous delivery address issued by Expo) and whether the device is Android or iOS.
  • Notifications waiting to be sent are queued on the server with their title and text (for example, "you're up next at this booth") until delivery succeeds or permanently fails.

On your device

  • Your signed-in session is stored on the device so you do not have to sign in every time. Signing out removes it.
  • Camera: used only to read booth QR codes. UpNext does not take, save, or upload photos or video, and the camera image never leaves your device.

What UpNext does not collect

UpNext contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not collect your location, contacts, photo library, calendar, microphone audio, advertising identifier, or any payment information, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. UpNext does not sell or rent personal data, and does not use it for advertising.

Why UpNext uses this data

  • To run the queue: place you in line, show your position, and call you when it is your turn.
  • To send the notifications you asked for.
  • To show recruiters and organizers who is waiting at their own booth or event, and nobody else's.
  • To keep the account secure and to answer your support emails.

Access is enforced on the server: students can read only their own tickets, and recruiters and organizers can read only the booths and events they belong to.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the UpNext database, authentication, and live updates, on infrastructure located in the United States.
  • Expo push notification service — delivers queue notifications to your device. It receives the device push token and the text of the notification.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivers notifications to Android devices, and Apple (APNs) does the same on iOS. These are the platform delivery channels the notification passes through.

These providers process the data on UpNext's behalf in order to run the service. UpNext does not share your data with anyone else.

Permissions UpNext asks for

  • Camera — to scan booth QR codes. You can decline and still join a queue with a booth code typed by hand.
  • Notifications — to tell you when your turn is coming. You can decline and still use the app; the queue updates on screen while UpNext is open.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, profile, and queue history are kept for as long as your account exists. UpNext does not currently delete old tickets automatically. You can have all of it deleted at any time — see Delete your account and data.
  • Push tokens are removed automatically when the delivery service reports that the device is no longer registered — for example after you uninstall UpNext — and when your account is deleted.
  • Queued notifications are held until they are sent or permanently fail, and are deleted with your account.
  • Backups. The database is backed up automatically by Supabase and those backups are rotated. Data you delete stays in an existing encrypted backup until that backup ages out of the rotation, after which it is gone. Backups are never used to restore an account that was deleted on request.
  • Support email. If you email the support address, that message stays in the support inbox as a record of the request and how it was handled.

Your choices

  • Delete everything — see Delete your account and data.
  • Turn off notifications — revoke the notification permission for UpNext in your device settings at any time.
  • Turn off camera access — revoke the camera permission for UpNext in your device settings.
  • Ask what is held about you, or ask for a correction — email the support address and say what you need.

Security

Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit, and the database is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Every queue action runs through server-side rules that check what your account is allowed to see and do. No system is perfectly secure, but UpNext is built so that one account cannot read another account's data.
