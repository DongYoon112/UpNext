What happens next

  1. Your request arrives at the support inbox.
  2. You get a reply asking you to confirm that you want the account deleted. This is the only check — confirming from the account's email address is all that is needed. You will never be asked for your password, and no payment or ID is involved.
  3. Once you confirm, the account and its data are deleted.
  4. You get a final email confirming that it is done.

Requests are handled by one person, not an automated system. Deletion is completed within 90 days of your confirmed request, and usually much sooner. If you have not heard anything, reply to your own email to bump it.

Deletion is permanent. Queue tickets, history, and your display name cannot be restored afterwards, and a new account started with the same email address begins empty.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account: email address and password hash.
  • Your profile: display name, role, and account creation date.
  • Every queue ticket belonging to you, with its full history — booths joined, ticket numbers, and the times you were called, checked in, cancelled, or expired.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices, and any notification still queued for you.
  • If you are a recruiter: your membership of every booth you belong to.
  • If you are an organizer or a company owner: the events, companies, and booths you created — and, because queue tickets belong to a booth, the tickets students hold at those booths. If you organized a fair that other people still rely on, hand it over or close it before requesting deletion.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups. The database is backed up automatically and those backups are rotated. Your data can remain inside an existing encrypted backup until that backup ages out of the rotation, after which it is gone. Backups are never used to bring a deleted account back.
  • Your deletion request itself. The emails you and the support address exchange stay in the inbox as the record that the request was made and carried out. Ask in your message if you want that thread deleted too once the deletion is finished.
  • Nothing else is kept. There is no analytics data, advertising profile, or copy of your queue history held anywhere else — UpNext does not collect any.

Only want to stop notifications?

You do not have to delete your account. Turn off the notification permission for UpNext in your device settings, or sign out in the app's Settings screen, and your account stays as it is.
