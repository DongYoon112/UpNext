Account and data deletion

Delete your UNBND account and data

This page is for UNBND, the career-fair virtual queue app (previously called UpNext, and before that CareerGOAT). There are two ways to delete your account. The first one is instant and is the one to use.

The fast way, in the app

Open UNBND → Account (top right) → Delete account.

The app first shows you exactly what will be removed — your active tickets, and, if you own companies or organise fairs, those and the queues attached to them. Confirm, and the account and its data are deleted immediately, not queued for review. You are signed out and returned to the sign-in screen. No email, no waiting, and you are never asked for your password again.

What happens next with an emailed request

  1. Your request arrives at the support inbox.
  2. You get a reply asking you to confirm. Confirming from the account's own email address is the only check. You will never be asked for your password, and no payment or ID is involved.
  3. Once you confirm, the account and its data are deleted.
  4. You get a final email confirming it is done.

Emailed requests are handled by one person, not an automated system. Deletion is completed within 90 days of your confirmed request, and in practice far sooner — the deletion itself takes seconds once the request is confirmed. If you have not heard anything, reply to your own email to bump it. Deleting in the app has no waiting period at all.

Deletion is permanent. Queue tickets, history, your headshot and your display name cannot be restored afterwards, and a new account started with the same email address begins empty.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account: email address and password hash.
  • Your profile: display name, role, major, the link to your headshot, and the account creation date.
  • Your headshot image file, removed from storage so its public address stops working.
  • Every queue ticket belonging to you, with its full history — booths joined, ticket numbers, and the times you were called, checked in, cancelled or expired.
  • Your row in every recruiter's student list, and every star any recruiter gave you. You disappear from their list and their next export.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices, and any notification still queued or logged for you.
  • If you are a recruiter: your membership of every booth you belong to, and the stars you gave students.
  • If you are an organizer or a company owner: the fairs, companies and booths you created — and, because queue tickets belong to a booth, the tickets students hold at those booths. If you organised a fair other people still rely on, hand it over or close it before deleting. The app shows you this count before you confirm.
  • If you called, checked in or talked to students as a recruiter, their tickets survive you — the record simply stops naming which recruiter it was.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Sign-in security records. The authentication service keeps an audit log of authentication events, such as sign-ups and sign-ins, and those entries can still contain the email address they belonged to after the account itself is gone. They are not visible to other users. If you want them cleared too, say so in your deletion request and they will be removed by hand.
  • Backups. The hosting platform takes automatic backups and rotates them. Your data can remain inside an existing backup until that backup ages out. Backups are never used to bring a deleted account back.
  • Anything a recruiter already exported. Recruiters can export their student list, including email addresses, as a CSV file to their own device. A file exported before you deleted your account is outside UNBND's reach and is not deleted. Ask that employer directly if you want it removed.
  • Your deletion request itself. The emails you and the support address exchange stay in the inbox as the record that the request was made and carried out. Ask if you want that thread deleted too once the deletion is finished.
  • Nothing else. There is no analytics data, advertising profile, or copy of your queue history held anywhere else — UNBND does not collect any.

Smaller changes you might want instead

  • Just remove your photo: Account → Change photo → Remove photo.
  • Just stop notifications: turn off the notification permission for UNBND in your device settings.
  • Just sign out: Account → Sign out. Your account stays as it is.

Your role cannot be changed. Deleting the account and creating a new one is the only way to switch between student, recruiter and organizer.
