A little guidance

Common questions

I'm not getting notified when my turn comes up

Open Account in UNBND and check that Queue alerts are on, then tap Send test. If the test does not arrive, check UNBND’s notification permission in your device settings and your network connection. Contact support if alerts still do not arrive. Your queue also updates live on screen whenever UNBND is open.

The camera won't scan a booth QR code

UNBND needs camera permission to read booth QR codes. If you declined it, turn it back on for UNBND in your device settings. Recruiters can also read out a booth join code you can enter by hand.

I signed up with the wrong role

Roles are fixed when the account is created, so a student account cannot become a recruiter account. Delete the account under Account → Delete account and create a new one with the role you want. Deletion is permanent and can affect queues you own; review the deletion details first or contact support.

Who sees my headshot and my email address?

Recruiters at the booths you join. Joining a booth's queue shows that booth's recruiters your name, photo, major and email address, and they can export that list. The Privacy Policy explains exactly who sees what, and how headshots are stored and deleted.

I want my account removed

Fastest: Account → Delete account in the app, which processes the deletion when you confirm. Some records and exported copies can remain; see the deletion page for details. Otherwise see Delete your account and data. You will never be asked for your password.
