Common questions

I'm not getting notified when my turn comes up

Open Settings in UpNext and tap Set up queue notifications, then Send a test notification. If the test does not arrive, notifications are turned off for UpNext in your device settings. Your queue also updates live on screen whenever UpNext is open.

The camera won't scan a booth QR code

UpNext needs camera permission to read booth QR codes. If you declined it, turn it back on for UpNext in your device settings. Recruiters can also read out a booth join code you can enter by hand.

I signed up with the wrong role

Open Settings and pick Student, Recruiter, or Organizer under Your perspective. You do not need a new account.

I want my account removed

See Delete your account and data. You do not need to reinstall the app, and you will never be asked for your password.
